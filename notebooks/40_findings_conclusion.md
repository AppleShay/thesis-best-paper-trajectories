# Notebook 40 — Findings & Conclusion

Winning a best paper award is associated with a persistent increase in annual citation flow, not just a one-time spike. The effect is visible for both career stages, but Senior authors benefit in absolute terms while Junior authors show proportionally larger relative gains. Publication rates stay flat, so the citation lift is about increased visibility and community attention to existing work — not just output growth. The effect holds across both ICWSM and JCDL, suggesting it's a robust property of best paper recognition in CS conferences generally.
